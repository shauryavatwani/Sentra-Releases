How this works
The AI engine tracks every person in frame with a multi-person pose model, then scores each pair on three signals: how close they are, how fast their limbs are moving, and whether hands are reaching into the other person's body space. An alert is raised only when the combined score stays high across consecutive checks — a single noisy frame, a handshake, or two people simply standing close together will not trigger it. People are named where the face is recognized, and listed as Unknown otherwise.
