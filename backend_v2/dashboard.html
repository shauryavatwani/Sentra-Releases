How this works
An approved visitor is recognised by the same face-matching pipeline as enrolled staff and students, using the photo taken at the gate. The engine keeps watching for them after their pass expires — that is the point — and raises an alert the moment they are seen on any camera. Repeat sightings of the same visitor are collapsed into one alert per hour, so a visitor standing in view does not flood the log. Alerts also appear in Smart alerts alongside altercation detection.
Add / update a person
Photos are saved to Faces/<name>/ and embedded with the same InsightFace pipeline as face_register.py. Each photo must contain exactly one clear face — anything else is skipped, never guessed.

How this works
The AI engine tracks every person in frame with a multi-person pose model, then scores each pair on three signals: how close they are, how fast their limbs are moving, and whether hands are reaching into the other person's body space. An alert is raised only when the combined score stays high across consecutive checks — a single noisy frame, a handshake, or two people simply standing close together will not trigger it. People are named where the face is recognized, and listed as Unknown otherwise.
